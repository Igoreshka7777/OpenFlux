OpenFlux router integration, installer and LuCI interface, 2026.

This package uses the OpenFlux csqtt transport from the existing OpenFlux source
tree (github.com/cacggghp/vk-turn-proxy module) and its Go dependencies. It is not
a new cryptographic protocol. Dependency copyright and licence notices are
distributed with the source bundle. The csqtt client is distributed under
GPL version 3, consistent with the transport module; see COPYING. OpenWrt,
LuCI, dnsmasq, nftables and Linux
are independent projects installed from the configured OpenWrt repositories.
